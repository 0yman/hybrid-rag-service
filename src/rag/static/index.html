      How are answers found?

      Your documents are split into short passages. Each question is matched two ways — by meaning, and by exact keywords — and the two rankings are merged. The best passages are then used to write the answer, and every sentence is tagged with the passage it came from. If the passages do not contain the answer, it says so instead of guessing.
